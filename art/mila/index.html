Mila

Back in the day when all the kool artists used to hang out in deviantART, I often joined art contests. One of them had me draw someone’s OC, Mila.

A little history:
I had several accounts there, one of which was Kyinn. It actually used to belong to an old friend of mine. She didn’t want it anymore and I thought the name was so cute, so she passed it on to me. Ah nostalgia, this was during my peak kawaii phase back in university.
