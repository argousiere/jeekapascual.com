---
title: "Sonnet XIII: The Map of Fractals"
date: 2008-02-21
tags: ["poetry", "sonnet"]
excerpt: "A variation of The Map of Constellations, but this time, looking at fallen leaves for wisdom."
---

Sorrow amidst my leaf-covered walkway  
Stagnant timeframes eternally staying  
In the dead leaves of tomorrow's longing  
Behold what mud-coated footprints convey

Bedecked by leaves that settle on the ground  
The earth breathing for the first time's ticking  
Clearing the path by my feet's mindless kicking  
So long been searching, but nothing is found

I trace along the paths of the leaves' veins  
From a source, several reasons exist  
Here, where only genius of minds have kissed  
Directs my fate made by fine choice of lanes

Each leaf which I lightly touch with my soles  
Each count, strong endearment for it to show
