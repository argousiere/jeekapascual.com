A gift for my mother-in-law who loves jonquils. She was born and grew up in a farm that housed an old flour mill in the Touraine region. Whenever spring came, she was beholden with the thousands of jonquils that bloomed near her childhood home.

Medium: Caran d'Ache Pablo colored pencils
