I revisited an old sketch that has been lying dormant in my archives since 2013, the year I started working.

Since entering the workforce, my art dwindled significantly. I decided 2023 was the year I rekindled my love for digital art, so I went out and procured myself an iPad. To ease up into drawing after almost a decade, I pulled out an old sketch of a space mechanic, which was inspired by Starcraft's Ghost operatives.
