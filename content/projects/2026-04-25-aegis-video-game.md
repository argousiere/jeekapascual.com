## My roles and contributions
Over the course of 5 months, I felt like I worked in an indie studio as I was able to wear many hats and contribute to multiple key areas:

- Gameplay Programming: Enemy AI, Inventory and equipment, Mineral spawning and distribution, Objectives and events handling
- UI Programming
- UX Designer
- 3D Modeler and Animation: Big Crawler
- Project Management - yes, coordinating across 2 timezones and different skill sets requires a role for that too!
