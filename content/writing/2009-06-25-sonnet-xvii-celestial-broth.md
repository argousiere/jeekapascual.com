---
title: "Sonnet XVII: Celestial Broth"
date: 2009-06-25
tags: ["writing"]
description: "Continuation of Sonnet XVI. Orbital gravity as a metaphor for unrequited love."
---

To be concealed within your stellar mind  
Is to hang dizzy from your spinning charm  
From which spun complex webs nourish and farm  
Old memories of yours I wish to find

To be imprisoned behind metal doors  
Is my fire to die by harsh domain  
My shadow there shall no longer remain  
Both swept away by your storms' dreadful force

To be released and exiled from this cell  
Is to be flung into the edge of space  
Then midway be caught by orbital lace  
And once again be entranced by your spell

Watch! For now I am your lunar guardian  
Held close yet held apart by attraction
