Accessible Concept Mapping in Miro

Georgia Tech TILES Lab

Jan 2026–Jul 2026

Miro is widely used in higher education, yet it remains largely inaccessible to blind and low-vision users despite ADA Title II requirements. This prototype is a Miro app that lets blind and low-vision users create and navigate concept maps. It runs alongside the whiteboard, so users never have to leave Miro.

Architecting Design Systems for Web Accessibility

University of Strasbourg · Master's thesis

Nov 2023

Comparative study of 5 design systems from the Open UI catalog and how they integrate web accessibility guidelines. Each is analyzed on the 4 components of information architecture and on the quality of its alignment with WCAG. The findings offer software teams practical guidance for building accessibility into their design systems.

The Road to a More Accessible Web: Bridging the Inequality Gap with AI

Graduate Inequality Review (University of Oxford), Vol. II

Jul 2023

Low web accessibility is an understudied dimension of the digital divide, affecting 1.3 billion people worldwide. The article argues that improving web accessibility benefits not only persons with disabilities but social welfare as a whole, and explores AI use cases that can help. It calls on governments, industry, and academia to invest in AI for accessibility to help close the global inequality gap.
